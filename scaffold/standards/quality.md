# Quality standards

Checked by the reviewer on every review, line by line.

## Defaults

- Every claim in a deliverable is verifiable: a reader can check it without taking the
  implementer's word for it.
- Every deliverable has a verification step, and the evidence for it lives in the work
  item's section 7.
- No silent scope growth: if the work needs more than the item's stated scope, the item is
  amended or a new one is opened — the extra work does not just happen.
