# Writing conventions

Applies to every output this team produces.

## Certainty tags

Tag claims by how well-supported they are:

- `[Certain]` — followed by the evidence.
- `[Likely]` — followed by the inference being made.
- `[Guessing]` — followed by what would settle it.

Untagged assertions are held to `[Certain]`: if it can't be backed up, tag it or cut it.
